# Code of conduct

Testfold contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces respectful, technical, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect different experience levels, identities, backgrounds, and communication styles.
- Protect private information and coordinate responsible disclosure of security issues.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, reviews, or other project spaces.

## Enforcement

For ordinary project disagreements, use the relevant issue or pull request and keep the discussion tied to the work. Report conduct that cannot be handled safely in public through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/witqq/testfold/security/advisories/new), marking it as a conduct report rather than a vulnerability. If that channel is unavailable, open an issue without private details and request a private contact channel.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of affected people.
